The Founding

It started with a window, a drafting board and a conviction.

In 1987, two architects rented a loft above a print shop on Harrison Street. They had one window, one drafting table, a coffee percolator and a conviction that the best neighbourhoods were built block by deliberate block.

That studio became Modern Town. Over four decades the team grew into planners, landscape architects, urban designers and engineers — but the rhythm has not changed. Every commission starts with a walk of the site, a long conversation about who lives there, and a pencil on a grid.

Our Values

Principles drawn on every sheet.

Precision Before Speed

A good plan takes time. We draw, test and redraw until the numbers match the lived experience — because a miscalculation costs a decade to undo.

Community-First Process

The people who live in a place know its problems better than any consultant. We design with them, not for them — and the plans are stronger for it.

Multi-Scale Thinking

We design rooms, buildings, blocks and districts as one system. A beautiful facade on a broken street is not a finished project.

"A city is not a machine. It is a living thing that grows at the speed of trust. Our job is to give it good bones and let the neighbourhoods fill in the rest."
— Studio Manifesto, 1987

Milestones

Four decades of built work.

1987

Founded on Harrison Street

Two architects, one drafting table, a percolator and a conviction that good cities are deliberate.

1995

First master plan commission

A 12-block district plan for downtown San Rafael — the commission that turned the studio from interiors to urban scale.

2004

Landscape & infrastructure team formed

Added in-house landscape architecture and civil engineering, completing the full-service studio model.

2013

64-acre master plan delivered

The largest single commission in studio history — a mixed-use district including parks, transit and thousands of homes.

2024

22 design awards & counting

Recognised by the APA, AIA and ASLA for projects that balance density with dignity.
