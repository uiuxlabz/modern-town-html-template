The Studio

Precision is the most beautiful part of a plan.

Modern Town began as a two-person drafting table in a converted warehouse loft. Today the studio spans planners, architects, landscape designers and engineers — but every commission still starts the same way: a pencil, a grid and a long conversation about how people will actually use the place.

We believe the best cities are neither utopias nor accidents. They are deliberate — drawn, tested, adjusted and drawn again until the street, the block and the building all agree.

What We Do

Four disciplines, one drawing.

01

Master Planning

District-scale plans that sequence growth over decades — land use, density, transport and infrastructure drawn as one coherent system.

02

Architecture

Buildings that earn their place — civic, residential and mixed-use design from concept sketch to construction documentation.

03

Community Development

Public space, affordable housing and civic infrastructure designed with the people who will use them — not just for them.

04

Infrastructure & Landscape

Streets, parks and utility networks that make density feel livable — the connective tissue of a functioning city.

06

Long-Range Visioning

Decade-scale studies for municipalities and developers — scenario modelling that helps cities grow deliberately.

How We Work

From field survey to ribbon cutting.

Survey & Listen

We walk the site, read the zoning and talk to the people who live there before a single line is drawn.

Sketch & Test

Options, not a single answer. We draw several futures and pressure-test each against cost and community.

Draw & Refine

Blueprints, sections and renderings refined through review cycles until every dimension earns its place.

Build & Steward

We stay through construction and beyond — because a plan that never opens is only a drawing.
